About this comparison

Singapore qualifying, 16 September 2023. Carlos Sainz, lap 19: 1:30.984. George Russell, lap 16: 1:31.056. Both match the Q3 times in OpenF1’s session results.

  • 341 Sainz samples and 340 Russell samples, sampled at approximately 3.7 Hz.
  • Lap and sector times are reported values. Estimated progress comes from integrating sampled speed and normalizing each recorded trace.
  • Brake indicates on or off, not pressure. These traces cannot establish the cause of a 72 ms lap-time difference.
